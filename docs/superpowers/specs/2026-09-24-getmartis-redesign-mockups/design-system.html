Code surface

Code is content, not decoration.

Motion180–700 ms, purposeful reveals and code-to-UI transitions. No looping glow, cursor spotlight or ornamental parallax. Full reduced-motion fallback.
AccessibilityWCAG 2.2 AA, visible focus, skip link, semantic landmarks, keyboard-safe lightbox, 44 px touch targets and contrast verified by tests.
PerformanceLCP ≤ 2.5 s, CLS ≤ 0.1, INP ≤ 200 ms. Responsive images, lazy media, route-level chunks and no WebGL requirement.
ResponsiveDesigned at 375, 768, 1024 and 1440 px. Product frames simplify rather than shrink into illegibility. Tables become guided comparison cards.
Photography & mediaOnly canonical Playground captures and short task-based clips. No stock people, fake dashboards or decorative 3D objects.
CopyEnglish, concise, agency-aware and evidence-led. Avoid “revolutionary”, “effortless” and unprovable superlatives.
Theme strategy: marketing stays deliberately dark for a consistent cinematic identity. Documentation follows the system preference and offers dark/light switching; the light mode uses Editorial Paper for long-form readability.
